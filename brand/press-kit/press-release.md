# AstraMindAI Launch Press Release

**Europe**

AstraMindAI today announced the launch of its AI platform, enabling teams to build safe, scalable, and deeply integrated AI experiences. The platform ships with:

- A multi-agent orchestration layer.
- A dedicated ML service for embeddings, intent classification, reranking, and toxicity detection.
- Production-ready blueprints for infrastructure, networking, and storage.

For more information, visit the AstraMindAI repository and documentation.
