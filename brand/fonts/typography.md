# Typography Guidelines

## Font Family

- **Primary:** Inter
- **Fallbacks:** system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif

## Scale

- Display: 32–40px, Inter Bold
- Heading: 24–28px, Inter SemiBold
- Body: 16–18px, Inter Regular
- Caption: 12–14px, Inter Regular

## Usage

- Keep line-height between 1.4–1.6.
- Avoid more than two weights per view.
